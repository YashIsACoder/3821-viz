Why circuits are hard to skip, and slow for Karp

95%of circuit SCCs have no cycle: little left to skip
92%of the cascade's time is Karp (median)

A cheaper comparison, tried outside the frozen code, made the cascade about 4× faster.

Caveats

  • One machine (Apple M2, 16 GB), best of three runs.
  • The generated families show when skipping can help. They aren't a sample of real graphs.
  • The five largest graphs ran only Howard and the hybrid: Karp's table didn't fit in memory.
