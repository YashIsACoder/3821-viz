What we proved

$\lambda(K)$: best loop inside SCC $K$ · $D(K)$: best gain after leaving · $\delta_k$: biggest one-row rise in Karp's table.

Soundness

The bounds never undershoot

$\lambda(K) \le \delta_{k+1}(K) \le \delta_k(K) \le M(K)$

Any bound $\le D(K)$ means skipping is safe.

Exactness

Skipping never changes the answer

$\text{cascade}(v) = g^*(v)$ for every state $v$

Same gains as plain Karp, always.

Running time

Pay only for what you solve

$O\bigl(n + m + \sum_{\text{solved}} n_K m_K + \sum_{\text{skipped}} k_K m_K\bigr)$

A skipped SCC costs only the rows it took to rule out. Worst case $O(nm)$, like Karp.

What was known, and what is ours

Known

  • Karp's algorithm, O(nm) Karp 1978

Ours
